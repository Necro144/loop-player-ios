# Third-party software

**opus-decoder** 0.7.11 by Ethan Halsall (https://github.com/eshaz/wasm-audio-decoders), MIT licence.
It bundles **libopus** (BSD 3-clause licence, Xiph.Org Foundation and others). Included unmodified as
`LoopPlayer/Resources/opus-decoder.min.js` and used to decode Suno songs on iOS versions that cannot play
Opus audio inside an MP4 file.
