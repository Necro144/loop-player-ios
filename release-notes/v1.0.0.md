## What's new in 1.0.0

First release.

- Pick one or more folders from iCloud Drive or On My iPhone. They are remembered, and subfolders are included.
- Plays MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, MP4 and MOV. Tags and artwork are read from the files, with the file name as a fallback.
- Songs, Albums, Artists, Folders, Videos, Favorites, Recently Played, Most Played and search.
- Loop one song, repeat all, shuffle, speed, sleep timer, queue (play next, reorder) and your own playlists.
- Background playback, lock screen and Control Center controls, AirPlay, and video with picture (full screen too).
- Double-tap to jump 10 seconds, swipe to change song.
- Picks up where you left off.
- Needs iOS 16 or later. Tested on an iOS 16.4 simulator, not yet on a real iPhone.

## Install

Download `LoopPlayer.ipa` below and install it with TrollStore, AltStore or Sideloadly. It is unsigned.
