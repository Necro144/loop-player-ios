## What's new in 1.1.0

**Video thumbnails**
- Songs that are videos now show a real picture from the video in the song list, the mini player, the Now Playing screen and on the lock screen / Control Center. Black opening frames are skipped.
- Videos that were still in iCloud or too slow to read during the scan get their picture afterwards (iCloud's own preview when the file isn't on the phone yet, a frame from the video once it is).

**Background playback that keeps going**
- The next song is now fully downloaded from iCloud while the current one plays. Before, the app could go silent while it fetched the next file, and iOS suspends silent apps in the background: the music stopped.
- Video songs turn their picture off when the app leaves the screen, so iOS no longer pauses them. If iOS still pauses the player on its own, it starts again.
- After a call or alarm the music comes back when iOS says it may. Settings > Folder Log now notes when and why playback was stopped (call, other app, headphones unplugged).
- New: Settings > **Play along with other apps** keeps the music going while another app plays sound (iOS then hides the lock screen controls).

**Speed with or without pitch**
- Tap the speed button on the Now Playing screen: any speed from 0.5× to 2× in 0.05 steps.
- **Keep pitch** (time-stretch, voice stays the same) or **Change pitch** (like a tape or a slowed video: slower sounds deeper). Also in Settings.

**A-B loop**
- Tap **A-B** at the start of a part, tap again at its end, and that part repeats. Tap a third time to stop. The part is marked on the seek bar.

**YouTube playlists**
- Playlists > **Import YouTube Playlist**: paste a playlist link. It plays in YouTube's own player inside the app, with next / previous, shuffle and repeat, and the list of videos with their titles.
- YouTube's rules: the video has to stay visible, nothing is downloaded, and it doesn't play in the background or with the screen locked. Public and unlisted playlists work, private ones don't, and up to 200 videos load. Videos whose owners blocked playing outside YouTube are skipped.

## Install

- **TrollStore:** download `LoopPlayer.tipa` and open it with TrollStore (install over the old one).
- **AltStore / Sideloadly:** use `LoopPlayer.ipa`.
