## What's new in 1.1.1

**Fixed: YouTube playlists stayed on "loading"**
- The player now loads the playlist in a way that makes YouTube send the video list right away, and the newest videos show at once from YouTube's public feed.
- If YouTube can't be reached or refuses to start its player, the screen now says so instead of loading forever, with Open in YouTube in the ··· menu.

**New: loop one YouTube video**
- Playlists > Add YouTube Playlist or Video: paste a link to a single video (watch, youtu.be or Shorts link) and it plays on a loop. The repeat button turns the loop off and on. Playlists also get repeat-one.

**New: import a Spotify playlist**
- Playlists > Import Spotify Playlist: paste a public playlist or album link. Loop Player copies the list of songs (names and artists), plays the ones it finds in your own files with no ads, and can save them as a normal playlist.
- Songs you don't have show a YouTube button that searches for them in YouTube.
- Spotify doesn't let other apps play its music, so nothing is streamed from Spotify, and no app can legally remove Spotify's or YouTube's ads.

**Fixed: videos without a picture**
- Pictures for videos are now made after the scan, without the time limit that big videos ran into, and also from the video itself the first time it plays. Lists, Search, playlists, the player and the lock screen all show them. Lengths that couldn't be read are filled in too.
- If a picture still can't be made, Settings > Folder Log says why.

**Fixed: the song stopped and started again when going to the home screen**
- 1.1.0 switched the video off a moment too late and then restarted the song. The picture is now taken off at the right moment, so the sound doesn't stop.

**Lock screen and Control Center**
- Loop Player now registers for the lock screen and Control Center controls when it starts, and sends the song info again as soon as the sound starts.
- Note: with Settings > "Play along with other apps" on, iOS hides the lock screen controls. Turn it off if you want them.

## Install

- **TrollStore:** download `LoopPlayer.tipa` and open it with TrollStore (install over the old one).
- **AltStore / Sideloadly:** use `LoopPlayer.ipa`.
