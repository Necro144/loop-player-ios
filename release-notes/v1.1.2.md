## What's new in 1.1.2

**Fixed: tapping a YouTube video said "the link is not valid, error 2" and Play did nothing**
- Loop Player now keeps its own list of the playlist's videos and gives YouTube's player one video at a time, instead of using YouTube's playlist mode that gave error 2. Next, previous, shuffle, repeat and repeat-one are handled by the app.
- Play starts the selected video even when nothing has loaded yet.
- ··· > Player Log shows what YouTube answered, if a video still won't play.

**Whole YouTube playlists (more than 200 videos)**
- Without a key, YouTube gives other apps at most 200 videos of a playlist. That limit is YouTube's.
- Settings > YouTube: paste your own free YouTube API key and Loop Player loads every video of the playlist (··· > Reload the List). The steps to get a key are written under the field.

**New: Clear Cache**
- Settings > Library > Clear Cache deletes stored pictures, YouTube's stored player data and downloaded web files, then makes the pictures again. Songs, playlists, folders and settings stay.

**Fixed: video pictures**
- A video's picture is now also taken from the frames the player shows while it plays, so a video gets its picture after you play it once, even when reading the file a second time doesn't work on the phone. Settings > Folder Log lists each picture made or missed.

**Fixed: no song on the lock screen and in Control Center**
- "Play along with other apps" makes iOS hide the song on the lock screen and in Control Center. It is switched off once in this update (you can turn it back on; its name now warns about this).
- Video files now keep playing as sound in the background, and the app keeps its sound session when you leave it.
- Settings > Playback > Lock Screen Check shows what iOS gets, if the song still doesn't show.

**About YouTube in the background**
- YouTube videos play only while Loop Player is on screen. YouTube doesn't allow other apps to play its videos in the background or on the lock screen (only the YouTube app with Premium can), so this can't be added. Your own songs and videos do play in the background.
