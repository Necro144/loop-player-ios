## What's new in 1.2.0

**New: play Suno songs from a link**
- Home > Play a Suno Link (also in Playlists > Suno): paste a Suno song link (suno.com/song/…, or a short suno.com/s/… link) and it plays right away.
- Paste a Suno playlist link to add and play the whole playlist.
- Songs stream from Suno the way suno.com plays them. Nothing is downloaded: only the cover picture is kept on the phone, and playing needs the internet.
- They play in the normal player, so loop, A-B loop, speed, background play, the lock screen and Control Center all work like they do with your own songs. A single song starts on repeat.
- Suno songs show on Home and in Playlists > Suno Songs. Swipe a song there to remove it (it stays on Suno). They can be added to your own playlists and favorites.
- Public songs only: Suno doesn't let anyone play a private song without signing in.
