## What's new in 1.2.1

**New: sign in to Suno to play your own songs**
- Settings > Suno > Sign in to Suno (also in Play a Suno Link). You sign in on Suno's own page inside the app; Loop Player never sees or keeps your password.
- Signed in, links to your private and unlisted songs play too. They still stream from Suno: nothing is downloaded.
- Loop Player tries, in order: the song's own audio, Suno's download link (only for songs already unlocked, so it never uses one of your downloads), then Suno's public video.
- If a link isn't public and you aren't signed in, the Suno screen now says so and shows a Sign in button. After signing in, the link plays at once.
- Settings > Suno > Sign Out of Suno removes the sign-in from the app.
- If a song still won't play, Settings > Folder Log lists what was tried.
