## What's new in 1.2.3

**Fixed: signing in to Suno didn't stick**
- Suno changed how its website signs people in (November 2026). 1.2.2 was still looking for the old way, so after you signed in with Google the app never noticed, and pasting a link asked you to sign in again.
- The app now asks Suno's sign-in service (auth.suno.com) the same way suno.com does. As soon as Suno shows its Create page after you sign in, the app shows "Signed in as …" and closes the sign-in screen.
- The token used to play your own songs comes from the same place, with a second way to get it if Suno's page doesn't answer.

**Fixed: Suno kept signing you out**
- After signing in to Suno, closing the app (or restarting the phone) often signed you out again, so private songs stopped playing.
- The Suno sign-in is now also kept safely on this phone (in the iPhone's Keychain) and put back every time the app starts. You stay signed in until you tap Settings > Suno > Sign Out of Suno.
- This was worse on TrollStore installs, where the app's web storage didn't always survive closing the app. That no longer matters.
- Settings > Clear Cache no longer signs you out of Suno.
- If Suno's page briefly shows no sign-in, the app now puts the kept one back and tries again before giving up, instead of signing you out at once.
- Loop Player still never sees or keeps your password: only Suno's sign-in cookies are kept.
- Settings > Folder Log shows when the sign-in was kept and restored.
