## What's new in 1.3.0

**New: a Suno tab**
- A sixth tab, Suno, opens suno.com inside Loop Player, like the Suno Legacy app: browse, create, open your library and playlists, and play songs there.
- Songs play on iOS 16 (the same Opus decoder as Suno Legacy), with the silent switch on and in the background.
- One sign-in for both: signing in on the Suno tab also signs in Settings > Suno (for pasting Suno links), and the other way round. The sign-in is kept in the iPhone's Keychain.
- Suno's page and Loop Player's own player don't play over each other: starting one pauses the other.
- The ··· button on the Suno tab opens a copied Suno link, goes to Suno's home page or reloads.
- Downloads from Suno open the share sheet, so you can save them to Files.
- The tab bar now has six buttons: Home, Library, Search, Playlists, Suno, Settings.

**Fixed: "Suno gave no playable file" for your own songs**
- While you're signed in, every Suno song is looked up again with your sign-in when it plays, whether it came from a link or a playlist.
- Your private songs only come as Suno's own audio format, which iOS 16 can't play by itself. When that happens the song now plays in the Suno tab, which can decode it, instead of failing.
- Settings > Folder Log now shows whether Suno accepted the sign-in, what Suno answered for the song, and the result for each file tried.
- The sign-in screen stops at once when you're signed in, instead of going on into Google again.

Includes everything in 1.2.3 (Suno sign-in fix).
