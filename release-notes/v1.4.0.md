## What's new in 1.4.0

**New: Share a Clip**
- A share button in the player (and in the full-screen video) makes a clip of 10 seconds, 20 seconds or 1 minute, starting where the video is playing now.
- The clip opens the iPhone share sheet: Instagram (Stories, Reels, Chats), Snapchat, WhatsApp, Telegram, Messages, Save Video, Save to Files and any other app that takes videos.
- Videos become an MP4 with picture and sound (H.264, plays everywhere). Songs without a picture become a sound clip.
- Near the end of a video, the clip takes the last part instead.
- A progress box shows while the clip is made, with Cancel.

Includes everything in 1.3.0 (Suno tab, private songs).
