## What's new in 1.4.1

**Fixed: Suno sign-in stuck**
- The sign-in is now kept the same way as in Suno Legacy: every change is saved, sign-in cookies are kept for a year, and when the app starts only missing ones are put back. 1.3.0 and 1.4.0 could put an old copy over a fresh sign-in, which left Suno's page half signed in and stuck loading.
- After signing in on the Suno tab, the page reloads once by itself if it still shows "Log in".

**YouTube in the background**
- YouTube videos keep playing when you leave the app or lock the screen.
- They show in Control Center and on the lock screen (title, picture, time), with play/pause, next, previous and seeking.
- Starting a song in Loop Player's own player takes the lock screen back.

**Share a Clip**
- A clip made while the song is slowed (or sped up) keeps that speed, and the pitch follows your "keep pitch" setting.
- New in the share menu: Instagram Story sends the clip straight into Instagram's story editor, without saving it to Photos. If Instagram doesn't open the editor, add a Facebook App ID in Settings > Share a Clip. Instagram takes story videos up to about 20 seconds.
- Snapchat, WhatsApp, Telegram and the rest: use Share.

Includes everything in 1.4.0.
