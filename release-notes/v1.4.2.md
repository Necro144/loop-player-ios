## What's new in 1.4.2

**Share a Clip: Snapchat Story**
- New in the share button: Snapchat Story (10 seconds, 20 seconds or 1 minute). Snapchat opens with the clip as a full-screen snap ("Snapchat pasted from Loop Player"), not as a chat attachment, and nothing is saved to Photos. Tap Stories to post it, or send it to friends.
- It uses Snapchat's own Creative Kit hand-over, the same way other apps send videos into Snapchat.
- If Snapchat says the app isn't allowed, add a free Snap Kit client ID in Settings > Share a Clip (the steps are written there).

Includes everything in 1.4.1.
