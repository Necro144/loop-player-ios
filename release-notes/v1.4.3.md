## What's new in 1.4.3

**Snapchat Story: "Something went wrong" explained and made easy to fix**
- Snapchat got the clip from 1.4.2 ("Snapchat pasted from Loop Player") but refused it: Snapchat only takes clips from apps that have their own free Snap Kit client ID, and Loop Player had none.
- Snapchat Story now asks for that ID first, with the steps, a button that opens kit.snapchat.com, and Paste Client ID (copy the ID, come back, tap it: the clip goes to Snapchat right away). It's asked only once.
- Clips for Snapchat are 720p, so the hand-over is about half the size.

Includes everything in 1.4.2.
