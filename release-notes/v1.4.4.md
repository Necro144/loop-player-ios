## What's new in 1.4.4

**Snapchat Story works with no setup**
- Loop Player now has its own Snap Kit app with Creative Kit turned on, and its client ID is built in: Share > Snapchat Story opens Snapchat with the clip ready to post as a Story. Nothing to paste.
- It works for the Snapchat accounts added as demo users of the Loop Player Snap Kit app. A client ID typed in Settings still wins over the built-in one.

Includes everything in 1.4.3.
