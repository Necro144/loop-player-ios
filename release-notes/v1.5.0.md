## What's new in 1.5.0

**Clip Studio** (the share button in the player, or Settings > Open Clip Studio)
- **Size:** Original, Story 9:16 (Snapchat, Instagram, TikTok, Reels, Shorts), Square 1:1, Portrait 4:5, YouTube 16:9, Cinematic 2.39:1, Classic 4:3. Fit with blurred bars, black bars, or fill (crop).
- **Length:** 5 s, 10 s, 15 s, 20 s, 30 s, 40 s, 45 s, 1 min, 1:30, 2, 3, 5 or 10 min, or any exact length. Start from here, from the start, or exactly your A-B loop.
- **Photos and videos from your library:** pick one and send it to Snapchat as a fresh snap (like one taken in Snapchat, not a Camera Roll upload), to Instagram Stories or anywhere.
- **Preview** of the clip's shape and look before making it.

**Fun**
- Looks: Vivid, Warm, Cool, Mono, Noir, Vintage, Chrome, Faded, Dream, 8-bit.
- Speed: 0.5x to 2x, Slowed 0.8x (deeper) or keep the pitch.
- Loop it: the part plays 2, 3 or 5 times inside the clip.
- Caption text, the song title, a Loop Player stamp, fade in and out.
- Songs become a song card video: their picture bouncing gently over a blurred background, with a moving progress bar, so Snapchat and Instagram take songs too (or choose Sound only).

**For developers**
- Every clip shows its details: picture size, frame rate, codec, bitrate, audio, file size and how long it took. Copy them as JSON.
- Recent clips: the last 12 are kept to send again, save to Files or delete.
- Quality: Auto, 720p, 1080p or 4K.
- Links for Shortcuts and other apps: `loopplayer://clip?seconds=30&size=story&look=mono&to=snapchat`, `loopplayer://studio`.

**Fixed**
- Instagram Story said "Instagram isn't installed" with a sideloaded Instagram (ReGram). Loop Player now opens Instagram without asking iOS first.

Includes everything in 1.4.4.
