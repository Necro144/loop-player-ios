## What's new in 1.5.1

**Song cards work**
- Songs without a video of their own become a real video clip for Snapchat and Instagram Stories: their picture over a blurred background, a moving progress bar, the title, and your caption.
- New card styles: Bounce, Spinning vinyl (the picture turns like a record), or Still.
- 1.5.0 couldn't make them and sent a sound file instead. Now every picture is drawn and written straight into the video, so iOS no longer refuses it.

**More in Clip Studio**
- Caption position (top, middle, bottom) and colour (white, yellow, pink, cyan, black). Emoji work in captions.
- A small buzz when the clip is ready.

**Steadier**
- Your Clip Studio choices are kept across updates (new options get their defaults instead of resetting everything).
- Cancel also stops a song card while it's being drawn.
- If a song card ever can't be made, the sound still goes out through Share, with the reason in the Player Log.

Includes everything in 1.5.0.
