## What's new in 1.5.2

**Clips work again on the iPhone**
- Making a clip of a video failed with "The clip couldn't be made: Cannot Complete Action" (or sat at "Making your clip…" forever). The Clip Studio effects were handed to iOS's video export, which crashed iOS's media service on the phone. Loop Player now paints every picture itself and writes the video itself, the same way song cards are made.
- If that ever fails, you still get a plain clip (the right part, speed and loops, the picture as it is), the way 1.4.4 made them.
- A clip that stops moving for 40 seconds is stopped with a clear message instead of hanging.

**Share and Preview**
- Share waits for a closing alert or sheet before opening, so the share sheet no longer gets stuck or doesn't show.
- Preview (and the preview picture in Clip Studio) works again; the preview picture is made in the background, so Clip Studio doesn't stall while you change options.

**Steadier**
- The main screen no longer redraws five times a second while a clip is being made.
- Clips are cut from their own copy of the file, not the one that's playing.
- Clips over 100 MB aren't pushed into Snapchat or Instagram (that could close Loop Player); pick 720p or a shorter length, or use Share.

Includes everything in 1.5.1.
