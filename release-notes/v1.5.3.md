## What's new in 1.5.3

**No more closing when you make a clip**
- 1.5.2 could close as soon as you tapped Snapchat (or Share) for a clip with a look or a new size. Loop Player now catches what iOS throws while it paints the clip and makes a plain clip instead of closing.
- If painting a clip ever does close Loop Player, the next clip is made the plain way automatically (the way 1.4.4 made them, which works on your iPhone). You'll see that in the Player Log.
- Clips with nothing to paint (Original size, Normal look, no text, no fade) are made the 1.4.4 way straight away.
- Songs without a picture of their own closed Loop Player in Clip Studio (1.5.1, "CILinearGradient"). Fixed: they get the Loop Player colour picture.

**Better reports**
- The Player Log now shows each step of making a clip, and if the app closes, which kind of error and where, so the next problem can be found straight away.

Includes everything in 1.5.2.
