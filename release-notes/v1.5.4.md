## What's new in 1.5.4

**Clips with looks and sizes work again**
- 1.5.3 still closed when it painted a clip (a crash inside Apple's Core Image on your iPhone, on the very first picture). Clips are now painted with Core Graphics and vImage instead, the same drawing tools the rest of iOS uses. Core Image isn't used anywhere in Loop Player any more: clips, song cards, photos, the Clip Studio preview and video pictures.
- Phone videos stored sideways are turned upright the same way the plain clip does it.
- If painting ever closes the app, the next clip is still made the plain way.
- New in Settings > Share a Clip: **Check clip painting** paints every look, shape and song card on your iPhone and says whether it all works (also written in the Player Log).

**Send to Snapchat button**
- A Snapchat button at the top left of Home, Library, Search, Playlists and Settings.
- Send a photo or video from Photos or from Files straight to Snapchat as a fresh snap (videos over 60 s are cut to 60 s), or a clip of what's playing (5 s to 1 min, with your Clip Studio choices).
- Also Open Clip Studio and Open Snapchat from there.

**Themes**
- Settings > Appearance: System (follows your iPhone), Light, Dark or Black (the old pure black look, the default).
- 10 colours for buttons and the playing song: pink, red, orange, yellow, green, mint, teal, blue, indigo, purple.

**Tested**
- Every tab in Light and Dark, Clip Studio, Send to Snapchat, every look and card style, a sideways phone video, 8-bit and Dream clips, plus all the earlier tests, on the iOS 16.4 simulator.

Includes everything in 1.5.3.
