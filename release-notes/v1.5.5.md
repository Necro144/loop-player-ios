## What's new in 1.5.5

**Real snaps on Snapchat**
- Snapchat marks every photo or video another app sends it as "Media Upload", whatever app sends it. A snap only shows as yours when it's taken with Snapchat's own camera.
- So Send to Snapchat (the yellow button at the top left) now has **Song sticker on the camera**: Snapchat opens its camera with the playing song (picture, title, artist) as a sticker. Move or resize it, then take the photo or video in Snapchat. It's a real snap.
- **Photos as real snaps** (on by default): a photo you pick goes onto Snapchat's camera as a sticker the same way. Videos are still sent as they are.

**Themes**
- System now really follows the iPhone's light and dark mode, and switches the moment you change it in Control Centre. Before, it could stay dark after the full player had been open.
- The full player stays dark in every theme (the artwork is behind it), without changing the rest of the app.

**Clear recent clips**
- Settings > Library > **Clear Recent Clips** (next to Clear Cache) shows how many clips and how much space, and deletes them. Also a Clear button under Recent clips in Clip Studio.

Includes everything in 1.5.4.
