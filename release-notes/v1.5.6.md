## What's new in 1.5.6

**Real snaps, no more "Media Upload"**
- Your test showed it: Snapchat labels a snap "Media Upload" whenever anything came in from another app through Snapchat's share kit, even a sticker put on its own camera.
- New default way, **Copy & paste**: Loop Player hands Snapchat nothing. It copies the song card (or your photo) and opens Snapchat's normal camera. Take your snap, tap **T** (text), hold on the screen and tap **Paste**. The picture goes on as a sticker, and the snap is a plain snap you took.
- Send to Snapchat (yellow button, top left) explains this before opening Snapchat.
- The old ways are still there under **Photos and stickers**: "Sticker on Snapchat's camera" and "Send the photo itself" (both show Media Upload).
- Videos you pick are still sent as they are (Media Upload): a video can't be pasted onto a snap.

Includes everything in 1.5.5.
